\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This project reproduced the Tuning-Free Plug-and-Play method of~\citet{Wei2022TFPnP} within the LION toolbox~\citep{Biguri2024LION} for 30-view parallel-beam sparse-view CT on LIDC-IDRI. The reproduction implements the RL policy-learning procedure, the ADMM environment, and the core baselines, and reproduces the paper's central claim, as TFPnP achieves $27.27$\,dB at 5\% noise, which is a $+2.96$\,dB improvement over hand-calibrated Fixed PnP-ADMM. An upstream contribution to LION (pull request~\#184) fixes a bug in the FBPConvNet integration surfaced during the reproduction.

The evaluation surfaces two findings the paper does not report. First, giving FBPConvNet in-domain training reverses its ranking against TFPnP at low noise ($+1.31$\,dB FBPConvNet advantage at 5\%), while TFPnP retains its advantage at 10\% across all three metrics, an iterative-robustness effect obscured by the paper's fully out-of-domain comparison. Second, a CT-specific DRUNet trained on LIDC improves Fixed PnP-ADMM by $+0.71$--$+1.98$\,dB, with the advantage growing under noise, and operates at a $5\times$ higher optimal $\sigma$ than the natural-image variant ($\sigma = 8$ vs $\sigma = 1.5$). Translating this gain into a fully retrained TFPnP policy encountered RL training instability. Our value network departs from the architecture specified by~\citet{Wei2022TFPnP}, so whether this instability is a property of TFPnP or of our implementation remains open.

Immediate follow-up work includes stabilising the CT-DRUNet policy retrain (narrower action range, gradient clipping, longer $\pi_2$ warmup, and restoring the weight-normalised TReLU value network of Table~1) and adding the RED-CNN, LPD, and RPGD baselines that were absent from LION, as well as retraining FBPConvNet on a mixed-noise regime to complete the fair-comparison analysis. Broader extensions include applying the infrastructure to alternative plug-and-play algorithms such as half-quadratic splitting~\citep{zhang2021plug} and regularisation by denoising, and generalising the geometry to fan-beam or cone-beam CT via LION's tomosipo backend. The wider lesson from this reproduction is that PnP methods demand careful denoiser calibration when the imaging domain shifts, and that reproducibility studies remain a valuable mechanism for surfacing methodological choices that primary papers leave underspecified.
